NeuralClear for Trusted Agents

Badge-gated escrow clearing for agent-to-agent micro-transactions on Concordium: many small obligations are netted off-chain, and only the net amount is paid once via x402.

This page shows one full settlement round produced by the project's offline demo (npm run demo, run on 2026-11-27). All amounts are testnet CCD with no real-world value. The payment leg shown here is the offline stand-in, not a chain transaction; live testnet settlement is pending the demo accounts described in the status section below.

In the full flow, both agents first verify each other's Verified by Concordium badge (Agent Registry, CIS-8004 / CIS-8) and refuse to trade if the badge is missing or revoked. The two agents' final Agent Cards have been generated with the official registry MCP and are hosted byte-for-byte in the public repository; on-chain registration is still pending (owner wallet signatures required).

Online settlement — the same round, settled on testnet (2026-11-27)

The identical batch (12 obligations, gross 0.77 CCD) was netted to a single 0.65 CCD transfer and settled for real on Concordium testnet through the project's self-hosted x402 facilitator, as a sponsored V1 transaction (buyer signs, facilitator sponsors the fee).
